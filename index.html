01. Introduction

Welcome to Slither & Climb: The Living Board ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of our users and their devices. This policy transparently outlines how data is handled when you download and play our Android game distributed via Google Play.

Summary for Players: Slither & Climb does not ask you for any personal account, email, or passwords. All match results, high scores, unlocked abilities, and settings are stored locally on your device.

02. Information We Do NOT Collect

Our game is engineered as a privacy-friendly, offline-first experience:

  • No User Accounts: We do not require registration, login credentials, or phone numbers.
  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect your name, email address, physical address, or contact list.
  • No Invasive Device Sensors: We never request access to your GPS location, camera, microphone, or storage contacts.
  • No Sensitive Financial Information: All gameplay elements and unlockables are contained entirely in-game without sensitive financial tracking.

03. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing independent development and keep the game free to play, Slither & Climb integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service operated by Google LLC.

When ads (such as optional rewarded revival ads or interstitial breaks) are requested, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically process standard non-sensitive device telemetry, such as:

  • Google Play Advertising ID (AAID): Used by Google to manage ad delivery, prevent fraud, and control ad frequency.
  • Coarse Location (IP-based): Used strictly by Google for coarse geographical ad targeting and regional compliance (not precise GPS).
  • Diagnostic & Performance Metrics: Crash reports, frame rates, and ad viewability statistics to help diagnose technical stability.
For complete technical details on how Google processes ad data, please visit the official Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

04. How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads

You maintain full control over advertising preferences on Android. If you wish to disable personalized ad recommendations across Google Play apps:

  • Open your Android device Settings app.
  • Navigate to Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads depending on your manufacturer).
  • Tap Delete Advertising ID or toggle on Opt out of Ads Personalization.

Once opted out, you will still see ads, but they will not be based on your online browsing profile.

05. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Slither & Climb does not intentionally target or collect personal identifying information from children under the age of 13. Our AdMob integration is configured according to Google Families and COPPA regulations. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that personal information was inadvertently provided, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly rectify the situation.

06. Data Security & Local Storage

Because we do not transmit personal player files or accounts to remote proprietary servers, your local game progression remains secure on your personal Android device. Any cache files or saved configurations can be wiped at any time by going into Android Settings → Apps → Slither & Climb → Clear Data.

07. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted directly to this public web page with an updated "Effective Date". We recommend reviewing this page periodically.
